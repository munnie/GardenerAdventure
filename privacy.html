Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Gardener Adventure: Vine Climb (the "App"), published by Munnie ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Information the App uses

  • Game progress. Your progress, coins, keys and purchased items are stored on your device. If you're signed in to Game Center and tap Save to iCloud, a copy of your save is stored in your Apple iCloud account so you can restore it on another device. We do not have access to this data.
  • Usage and diagnostics (Google Analytics for Firebase). To understand how the game is played and improve it, the App sends anonymous gameplay events, for example tutorial started, level started or finished, score, coins earned or spent, and screens viewed. Firebase also collects technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, country (derived from IP address), and an app-instance identifier. This data is not used to identify you personally.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob). If you choose to watch an optional video for a reward, Google AdMob serves the ad. AdMob may collect device information, IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. The App does not request permission to track you across apps (App Tracking Transparency), so your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The App receives a transaction record from Apple to deliver what you bought, and purchase events (product and price) are recorded in analytics.

How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and deliver purchases.
  • To understand which levels are too hard or too easy, fix bugs and improve the game.
  • To show optional reward videos.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

These services process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps
  • Apple (App Store, Game Center, iCloud): apple.com/legal/privacy

Data retention

Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Game progress stays on your device and in your iCloud account until you delete the App or its iCloud data.

Your choices

  • You can play without watching any ads.
  • You can reset your advertising settings in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising.
  • Deleting the App removes all locally stored data. You can remove the iCloud copy in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • You can ask us about, or ask us to delete, any data associated with your app instance by contacting us below. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete your data.

Children

The App is a cartoon game suitable for a general audience, but it is not directed specifically at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent to the services above over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use the protections those services provide.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
