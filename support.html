Support

We're happy to help with anything in Gardener Adventure: Vine Climb.

Contact us

Email pokobuilder@zohomail.eu. Please include your device model, iOS version, the app version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How to play

  • Move: hold the left or right buttons.
  • Jump: tap JUMP.
  • Shoot a vine: tap FIRE. Vines trap enemies and act as platforms you can stand on.
  • Drop a vine: jump on top of a vine to break it and send it falling onto enemies below.
  • Reach the top of each stage before the rising water catches you.
  • New to the game? Open Settings and tap TUTORIAL or HOW TO PLAY at any time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock new worlds?

Collect all seven colored seeds in a world, then beat that world's boss (stage 5). You'll earn a magic key that opens the next world. World keys can also be bought in the shop.

How do I get the seeds?

Seeds appear during stages. Defeat enemies in the right way and keep an eye out; if you miss one, replay the stage to try again.

What are coins for?

Coins buy permanent upgrades in the shop, such as Wings for extra mid-air jumps, and can pay for a second chance during a run.

What is a Life Sprout?

A Life Sprout gives you one extra chance per run without watching a video.

I bought something but didn't get it.

Open the shop and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Consumable items such as coin packs can't be restored; if one didn't arrive, contact us with the date of purchase.

How do I get a refund?

All payments are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Back up your progress first: open Settings and tap SAVE TO iCLOUD (you need to be signed in to Game Center). On your new device, sign in with the same Apple ID and Game Center account, open Settings and tap LOAD FROM iCLOUD. Purchases are always kept and can be restored from the shop.

Does the game have ads?

There are no forced ads. You can choose to watch a short video for an extra chance, or skip videos completely by using a Life Sprout.

Is the game suitable for children?

Gardener Adventure is a cartoon platformer for all ages. In-app purchases can be turned off with Screen Time on your device (Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions).
