One very strange seed

In a little village lives a gardener with a special gift: with a flip of her hand, the garden blooms. One day a stranger brings her an unusual seed. A little soil, a little water, and a LOT of growing later, she is carried into the sky and lands in a strange land far from home.

Now she has to climb, jump and grow her way back.

Shoot vines, climb higher

Throw magic vines to trap enemies, then jump on them and use them as bridges. Break a vine to send it crashing onto the monsters below.

Outrun the rising water

Every stage is a race to the top. The water keeps rising below you, so keep climbing!

Chain combos

Defeat enemies in quick succession for chain bonuses, and go for a SUPER CHAIN.

Boss battles

Each world has four climbing stages and ends in a boss battle.

Collect all seven seeds

Find the seven colored seeds in a world and defeat its boss to earn a magic key that opens the next world.

Power up

Speed shoes, potions, a guardian fairy and more. Earn coins for permanent upgrades like wings for extra mid-air jumps.
